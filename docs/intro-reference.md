# Opening-title references

Reviewed October 5, 2026.

- [Erik Buth — How I Met Your Mother](https://erikbuth.com/how-i-met-your-mother): the original designer and director's portfolio, including the finished title stills and exploratory storyboards. Buth describes shooting background photography with a Lomo camera in a bar.
- [Buth's original title-design project on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/12367461/How-I-Met-Your-Mother-title-sequence-and-logo): the title sequence, logo explorations and test storyboard.
- [Original and alternate openings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8lguc5Ota4U): reference for the order and rhythm of the photo edit.

## What carries into this adaptation

The finished stills show warm amber/yellow-green bar photography, dark edges, candid clusters of friends and loose white serif lettering. The name sits above the cast in an early photograph, arranged diagonally as “how i met / your / mother.” The creator credit belongs to the final close group portrait. This differs from a tiled photo wall with a centered sans-serif logo.

The app stages six original photographs with its own procedural cast and bar. Each is rendered once, with space between faces, then held while the print pans slightly. Brief blurred reframes connect the photos. The app name uses the reference's lowercase serif treatment; creator credits stay clear of faces. The final photograph cuts directly to the episode, where the cast credits roll.

The synthesized theme remains an original melody: six bars of guitar, bass-register strums and drums, with a ringing finish. The edit and music share a 0.2-second eighth-note pulse, 48 pulses and a 2.4-second tail (about 12 seconds), including when music is disabled. This is an adaptation of the opening's feel, not a claim to reproduce its exact musical or shot timing.

No reference photography or recordings are included in the app. Reduced-motion playback removes photo movement, rotation and blur. Playback time drives the edit so pausing freezes it, and the title overlay and music are cleared when skipped.
