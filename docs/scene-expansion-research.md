# Scene expansion research

Implementation follow-up: the user selected MacLaren’s sidewalk, the Hoser Hut, the courtroom, Atlantic City casino/arrival, and the Lusty Leopard. These are now implemented; see [the visual reference log and preview instructions](../README.md#more-locations-from-the-show). The recommendations below preserve the initial research.

Researched 6 October 2026. Original scope: additional playable locations and the kinds of scenes they could support. These are proposals, not new episodes or implemented sets. Scene pitches below are original; episode references describe the original HIMYM.

The local show bible supports a warm, retrospective ensemble comedy with elaborate bits, affectionate friction, and occasional sentiment. There are already 18 story locations, including the subway, laser-tag arena, Wesleyan dorm, hospital, elevator, and Canadian mall. The best additions give particular characters a home territory or give conversations a new physical situation.

**Recommended first batch: the sidewalk outside MacLaren's, Lily's classroom, and the Hoser Hut.** Add Marshall and Lily's apartment next for sustained domestic stories; a courtroom for Marshall's professional life; Farhampton for occasional reflective scenes.

## 1. MacLaren's sidewalk and apartment stoop

**Why it belongs:** Arrivals, interrupted departures, walking home, waiting for a cab, and conversations that continue after everyone else leaves are useful connective scenes. The project already has the shared pub/apartment facade, but it is currently establishing scenery without actor marks. Turning that familiar frontage into a playable set would connect the existing interiors.

**References:** Pamela Fryman's [DGA interview](https://www.dga.org/craft/dgaq/issues/1001-spring-2010/profile-pamela-fryman) discusses the moving street staging of the two-minute date in “Ten Sessions” (S3E13). Stephan Olson's [production-design portfolio](https://www.stephanolson.com/himym) includes the pub exterior, alley behind the bar, and snowy New York street. The [street photograph](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/dea72c_d39580aa16e44cad8eec14177899a4e9~mv2.jpg) was visually inspected: compact storefronts, streetlamps, subway stairs, and seasonal dressing.

**Original scene pitch:** Ted and Robin discover that each adds several blocks to the walk home to avoid ending a good conversation. Barney offers to optimize their route, then makes it longer because he wants company too.

**Build shape:** A compact sidewalk with pub stairs, residential stoop, and a curbside conversation area. Start with actors stopping at distinct marks; continuous walking dialogue would need separate investigation. Adapt the existing facade and provide street-facing reverse backgrounds. Winter decoration can be a later variant.

## 2. Lily's kindergarten classroom

**Why it belongs:** Lily becomes the competent host; the others enter a world where their adult status counts for very little. It also gives Scooter a natural workplace connection and supports parent nights, classroom preparation, and school fundraisers.

**References:** “Monday Night Football” (S2E14) puts Marshall in Lily's classroom while he tries to avoid hearing the Super Bowl result. See the [episode listing](https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/monday-night-football/umc.cmc.2kj2gzurrf0odooxdbxvjahmo?showId=umc.cmc.hrenotb7pqaz2mo61vph1gwj), [scene recap](https://johnautry24.wordpress.com/2014/02/20/himym-review-season-2-episode-14-monday-night-football/), and [inspected episode still](https://johnautry24.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/monday_night_football_01.jpg). Visible details include a green chalkboard, low round table, small colored chairs, books, an abacus, and a large yellow teaching clock. The still is hosted by a fan review, not an official production archive.

**Original scene pitch:** The gang helps Lily prepare for an open house. She uses a classroom reward chart to organize them, and Ted and Barney become fiercely competitive over who gets to be line leader. The payoff is Lily recognizing a friend's effort when she thinks nobody is watching.

**Build shape:** Teacher's area, low table, craft storage, and doorway. Adult-only preparation scenes work immediately with the existing cast. Seated adults on tiny chairs need deliberate pose/framing work; a full class of children would require additional character support.

## 3. The Hoser Hut

**Why it belongs:** This lets Robin enjoy a community where she belongs. Marshall can understand her homesickness; Barney and Ted become the visitors who misunderstand the room's social rules. Give Robin friends and interests beyond being teased about Canada.

**References:** “Little Minnesota” (S4E11) leads from Marshall's Minnesota bar to Robin's Canadian refuge. [Apple's episode listing](https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/little-minnesota/umc.cmc.6jvaiu3i61l87btd38w5760bg?showId=umc.cmc.hrenotb7pqaz2mo61vph1gwj) establishes the Marshall/Robin bar story; [Myles McNutt's analysis](https://cultural-learnings.com/2010/02/08/a-canuck-in-an-american-sitcom-the-spatial-construction-of-canadian-identity-in-how-i-met-your-mother/) explains the Hoser Hut's role and its return in “Duel Citizenship.” The [location reference](https://how-i-met-your-mother.fandom.com/wiki/Hoser_Hut) describes maple leaves, Canucks shirts, and karaoke. These are secondary references; exact floor plan and colors still need an episode-frame check before modeling.

**Original scene pitch:** Robin takes the gang to her regular trivia night, where everyone knows her as the patient, cheerful one. She spends the evening trying to keep her New York friends from damaging her reputation while quietly losing her patience with them.

**Build shape:** Sports-viewing table, bar, and small karaoke corner. Reuse furnishing helpers while giving this room its own arrangement and focal point. Any performed song should be original, consistent with the bible's Robin Sparkles guidance.

## 4. Marshall and Lily's own apartment

**Why it belongs:** A separate home lets the couple set the terms of hospitality. Hosting, household purchases, visiting parents, privacy, and who gets a spare key become stories about their marriage. It also offers Lily an art-making space.

**References:** Olson's [portfolio](https://www.stephanolson.com/himym) labels photographs of their [living room](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/dea72c_d777ae70b5c54b67bdcdf0800ac881f4~mv2.jpg) and [kitchen](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/dea72c_aba2b698a2e74f169e30090d9072bb26~mv2.jpg). Both were inspected: mustard sofa, dark trim, bay-window art area, and a cream kitchen with an island. Use these as the visual baseline; check episode continuity before assigning a specific move-in date or address.

**Original scene pitch:** Marshall and Lily host a dinner to prove they are a self-sufficient household. Ted brings an unsolicited seating plan and emergency supplies, then realizes he is trying to preserve his role in their lives. They give him one small job that matters.

**Build shape:** Living-room group, kitchen island, dining area, and entry. Design distinct master shots for hosting and private kitchen conversations. Establish who lives here in each story, since the project's timeline is intentionally loose.

## 5. A courtroom and courthouse waiting area

**Why it belongs:** Marshall gets to demonstrate professional competence under pressure. Brad and Arthur already exist in the cast, and the gang can provide wildly unhelpful moral support. A waiting-area scene also lets a verdict or hearing remain offscreen.

**References:** [“Twelve Horny Women” (S8E8)](https://tv.apple.com/gb/episode/twelve-horny-women/umc.cmc.5lencmwg6xz25i3rmf2587wjd?showId=umc.cmc.hrenotb7pqaz2mo61vph1gwj) places Marshall against Brad in a major case. The existing [cast reference](cast-reference.md) and README also link a courtroom still for Brad. The episode establishes the setting's narrative fit; the following layout is a proposal, not a verified floor plan.

**Original scene pitch:** Marshall rehearses a concise statement with the gang. Ted keeps adding context, Lily anticipates every objection, and Barney demands a dramatic entrance. At the hearing Marshall discards all their flourishes and says the simple thing he believed at the start.

**Build shape:** Judge's bench, counsel tables, witness position, and modest gallery. A smaller waiting-area set could come first. Keep the comedy on preparation, friendship, and professional rivalry; courtroom scenes should still feel like this sitcom.

## 6. Farhampton station

**Why it belongs:** A waiting bench and a departure deadline create space for intimate two-person scenes. It supplies a quieter visual rhythm for stories about timing, friendship, and missed opportunities.

**References:** Olson's [portfolio](https://www.stephanolson.com/himym) includes two platform photographs. The [inspected production photograph](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/dea72c_2c2611e419fb47cfb8e42e4312093f45~mv2.jpg) shows a pale clapboard station, curved canopy supports, bench, warm windows, and a wet platform. [“Farhampton” (S8E1)](https://how-i-met-your-mother.fandom.com/wiki/Farhampton) supplies the episode context. The photograph documents the actual stage; it is not a real Long Island station reference.

**Original scene pitch:** Ted calls a missed train fate; Robin calls it a missed train. As he constructs increasingly elaborate explanations, she stays and waits with him despite having another way home. Let the action carry the affection without an explanatory closing moral.

**Build shape:** Bench pair, sheltered standing area, platform entrance, and a track-facing reverse. Train sound and an offscreen arrival can carry the departure. Rain is optional additional work. Reserve this place for purposeful visits, and keep stories compatible with the bible's ongoing search rather than replaying the mother reveal.

## Later additions

- **Eriksen family kitchen in St. Cloud.** [“Belly Full of Turkey” (S1E9)](https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/belly-full-of-turkey/umc.cmc.27isjwkock1ze83na6egm94qz?showId=umc.cmc.hrenotb7pqaz2mo61vph1gwj) establishes visits to Marshall's family. Judy and Marvin Sr. already exist. Explore Marshall reverting to his childhood role or Lily finding an unexpected ally; the local “The Hotdish Accords” already uses a casserole rivalry. Interior art direction remains to be researched.
- **Farhampton Inn lobby/lounge.** A [2014 set-visit gallery](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/how-i-met-your-mother-200th-episode-set-visit-slideshow.html) offers a reference starting point. Useful for wedding guests, family weekends, and private conversations beside public festivities. Treat it as a separate set from the station.
- **World Wide News newsroom.** Olson's portfolio has dedicated WWN desk and bullpen images. This could support Patrice and Sandy in a distinct later-career setting; Metro News One is already playable, so the incremental story value is lower than the first batch.

## Authenticity and implementation notes

Fryman's [DGA interview](https://www.dga.org/craft/dgaq/issues/1001-spring-2010/profile-pamela-fryman) describes comedy created through editing, retellings, and active background staging. Apply that principle to these additions: a location should supply an entrance, interruption, reveal, or change in status, as well as a recognizable backdrop.

Give each room a few readable identity cues at the app's low resolution, then protect facial visibility and reaction shots. Build usable entrances and clear paths. Every implemented set needs wide, close-up, two-shot, over-the-shoulder, and applicable reverse-angle verification in the running app, with `mute` in the URL and representative screenshots, per AGENTS.md.

Primary visual evidence is strongest for the sidewalk, Marshall/Lily apartment, and Farhampton. The classroom has an inspected episode still. Hoser Hut, courtroom, and the later additions need further visual coverage before anyone claims a faithful reconstruction. Reference photographs remain research material; this proposal does not add them to the app or change episode JSON.
