# Home base and special locations

Research checked 7 October 2026. The observations below inform our location choices. Keeping special locations
out of the main-setting role and limiting visits to one or two brief scenes is our writing policy, requested
for this project; it is not a measured frequency or a universal rule from the original show.

## Plan locations before drafting

- **Anchor the episode.** MacLaren's and the apartment are home base. Other everyday settings such as workplaces,
  a restaurant or the sidewalk can carry scenes when the characters' lives take them there. The rule does not
  require every scene, opening or ending to happen in the same two rooms.
- **Keep special destinations supporting.** Laser tag (`laser_tag`) and the Hoser Hut (`hoser_hut`) must not be
  the main location. Apply the same restraint to other novelty destinations such as the Atlantic City casino,
  Canadian mall and Lusty Leopard. Keep special-location material to one or two brief scenes in total across
  the episode, only when the story benefits; zero is normal. The main conflict must develop in regular settings,
  with more than a token bar scene surrounding a destination-centered episode.
- **Give the visit a job.** Identify the complication, reveal or payoff that needs this place, then leave when
  it lands. A request to feature a venue can be satisfied by one strong scene. Move interchangeable exposition,
  arguments and debriefs to home base. A new set or a low catalog count is not a story reason.
- **Space visits across episodes.** Read recent episodes and compare the current batch before assigning a
  special destination. Avoid consecutive returns by default; don't rotate different novelty venues into every
  episode either. There is no obligation to use every set or give special locations equal exposure.
- **Review the full footprint.** Include cutaways, nested visits, resumed scenes, replays, split screens and
  montage shots. Two long scenes or many tiny returns can still make a special venue dominate the episode.
  Judge dialogue/action and story weight as well as scene count. `bun run episodes list` shows top-level sets;
  use `bun run episodes read <file>` and inspect the JSON for the complete location use.

For example, an original story about Barney treating friendship like a ranking system could use one short
laser-tag scene to expose what his system costs him, with the argument and fallout at MacLaren's and the
apartment. A Robin story could earn a brief Hoser Hut visit when feeling understood changes her next decision.
Neither needs the gang to spend the episode at that destination. These are examples, not mandatory structures.
